What we offer

Founder independence, succession, governance — enduring organizations.

LegacyOS™ Consulting helps you build the systems, structures, and culture your organization needs to thrive beyond your leadership. We assess your current dependencies, design governance frameworks, develop leadership pipelines, and transfer critical institutional knowledge.

Whether you're planning for succession, reducing founder dependency, or building a board that provides real oversight — we provide the expertise and frameworks to make your organization enduring.

Our consulting services

What we help you build and improve

Each engagement is tailored to your organization's size, complexity, and strategic goals. We bring the framework — you bring your vision for the future.

Founder Dependency Assessment™ — measure how dependent your organization is on its founder and identify critical gaps.
Founder Mortality Test — a practical exercise to determine whether your organization could survive a sudden leadership absence.
Institutional Knowledge Audit — identify where critical knowledge resides and how to capture and transfer it.
Succession‑readiness assessment — evaluate your leadership pipeline and prepare for orderly transitions.
Governance design — build board structures, advisory councils, and decision‑making frameworks that provide oversight and direction.
Leadership Pipeline development — create a systematic approach to identifying, developing, and placing future leaders.
Process documentation — document critical workflows, policies, and procedures to preserve institutional knowledge.
Culture‑transfer strategy — ensure your organization's values and culture are intentionally passed to new leadership.
Ninety‑day LegacyOS™ implementation — a rapid‑start program to get your continuity systems in place quickly.
Family‑business continuity planning — specialized support for multigenerational and family‑owned enterprises.
