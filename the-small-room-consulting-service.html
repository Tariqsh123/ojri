What we offer

Fair hearings, credible investigations, defensible decisions.

Our Small Room Consulting helps organizations build and implement fair, transparent, and legally defensible processes for workplace investigations, hearings, and decision-making. We work with you to assess current practices, design better systems, and train your teams to apply The Small Room Method with confidence.

Whether you need to review a single investigation, design a new hearing process, or build a culture of procedural fairness across your organization — we provide the expertise and frameworks you need.

Our consulting services

What we help you build and improve

Each engagement is tailored to your organization's needs, size, and sector. We bring the framework — you bring your context.

Workplace‑investigation review — assess existing investigation practices, identify gaps, and recommend improvements.
Investigation planning — design investigation plans that are thorough, fair, and defensible from start to finish.
Procedural‑fairness assessment — evaluate your current processes against best practices for fairness and due process.
Credibility‑analysis training — train investigators and decision‑makers in effective, defensible credibility assessment.
Hearing and decision‑making process review — review hearing procedures, decision‑making frameworks, and documentation quality.
Investigator and decision‑maker training — equip your team with the skills to conduct fair, thorough investigations and hearings.
Organizational trust assessment — measure how your processes affect trust in leadership and institutional confidence.
Policy and process evaluation — review and revise your policies to align with procedural fairness and legal best practices.

How we work

From assessment to implementation — a clear path forward.

Our consulting engagements follow a structured yet flexible approach. We begin by understanding your current challenges and organizational context. Then we design a tailored plan that may include assessment, training, process design, and implementation support.

We work alongside your team — not as external evaluators, but as partners committed to building fairer, more trustworthy institutions. Our methods are practical, evidence‑based, and designed to be sustained long after our engagement ends.
